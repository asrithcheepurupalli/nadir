Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

NADIR is an app that names what is on the ground below you, from your phone's sensors, with an honest error margin. This page explains, in plain words, what nadir.made-by-ac.com collects and what we do with it. The answer is very little, and your location stays on your device.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us, and the app itself does not send us anything.

  • Location and motion: when you use the app, it reads your phone's location and orientation sensors on your device to work out where you are aiming. Those readings are used on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The waitlist box: if you type an email address into it, the address is kept in your browser on your device. There is no waitlist server yet, so it does not reach us. When there is one, this page will say so before anything changes.
  • If you email us, we receive your message and your address. Email providers have their own privacy terms.

What we do not do

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not run analytics on this site, we do not build profiles of visitors, we do not track your location and we do not sell or rent anyone's details.

What the site stores in your browser

The app stores a small waitlist entry in local storage, and a service worker saves the app and its map data on your device so it works offline, such as on a flight. You can clear both in your browser's site settings.

Who handles it for us

A few providers process data so the site works. They act on our instructions:

  • Vercel hosts the website. Like any host it processes standard request data (IP address, browser, pages requested) in server logs. Its servers can be outside India.
  • Fonts: this site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so when a page opens your browser asks Google's font servers for them, and Google can see your IP address and browser details in that request. We do not send Google anything else.

How long we keep it

We only hold what you email us, for as long as we need it to reply. Ask and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, by writing to thebrain@made-by-ac.com. We reply ourselves. If you are in a country with data protection rights of its own, those apply too and we will honour them.

Changes and contact

If what this site does with data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions, requests and complaints go to thebrain@made-by-ac.com.
